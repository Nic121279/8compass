Weather & wind

False Bay is often cooler than the city, and the summer south-easter can hammer the deck and the canal. Check wind before you take any craft out. Winter evenings: use the fireplace, close the sliders. Swimsuits plus a jersey is normal here.

House rules

By booking and staying at 8 Compass Close you agree to these rules. They keep the home comfortable for you, for neighbours on East Lake Island, and for the next guests.

1. Check-in and check-out

  • Check-in: 15:00 or later. Early check-in may be possible if the home is ready — please ask at least 24 hours ahead (a small fee may apply).
  • Check-out: 10:00 sharp. Late check-out may be charged.
  • Self check-in: Use the smart-lock code in your Airbnb welcome message. Do not share it outside your booked party.
  • On departure: lock all doors and windows, switch off lights and appliances, and return keys to the lockbox as instructed.
  • The home: 3 bedrooms, a swimming pool, a single garage and driveway parking.
  • Guest limit: Only the guests on your booking. Maximum occupancy is 6. Extra visitors need prior approval and may attract a fee.

2. Smoking and substances

  • No smoking of any kind inside (cigarettes, cigars, vapes). Outside only, at least 4 metres from doors and windows. Use the ashtray — do not litter.
  • No illegal substances on the property. Breach means immediate eviction without refund and may be reported.

3. Parties, events and noise

  • No parties, events or gatherings beyond the booked guests. No DJs, speakers on the lawn, or amplified setups.
  • Quiet hours: 22:00 – 07:00 every day. East Lake Island is a quiet residential marina. Keep voices, TV and music down at all times.
  • Noise complaints can lead to fines or eviction.

4. Pets

  • No pets, including visiting animals. Service animals only with written pre-approval and documentation.
  • Fur, damage or evidence of an animal will attract a deep-cleaning fee.

5. Cleanliness and care

  • Treat the home as you would your own. Wash and put away dishes. Do not leave food out (it attracts pests and gulls).
  • Refuse: general waste collection on Mondays. Recycling: Wednesdays. Use the correct bins.
  • A standard clean is included. Extra mess, stains or overflow bins may be billed.
  • Report breakages immediately via Airbnb. Do not rearrange furniture or stick anything to the walls.

6. Amenities

  • Kitchen, laundry, garden and pool are yours to enjoy — clean as you go; do not overload the washing machine.
  • Parking: one car in the single garage and additional cars on the driveway only. Do not block the cul-de-sac or neighbours. Street parking is at your own risk.
  • Pool: unsupervised swimming is at your own risk. The pool sits on the wooden canal deck and is not a certified child barrier. No glass in or around the pool. No diving. Children must be watched at all times. Do not add chemicals or empty the pool.
  • Braai / fireplace: use the outdoor braai and indoor fireplace with care. Never leave a fire unattended. Douse coals fully. Do not burn rubbish.
  • Trampoline: own risk; one child at a time if possible; no shoes or somersaults onto hard surfaces.
  • Keep gates closed. The marina waterways are not childproofed.
  • Cape Town water is precious — short showers, please. Switch off lights, heating and air-con when you go out.

7. Safety and neighbours

  • Emergencies: 10111 (SAPS) or 10177 (ambulance). Fire extinguisher and first-aid kit are marked in the house.
  • East Lake Island has controlled vehicle access and 24-hour security. Still lock up when you leave. Do not invite strangers onto the property.
  • Supervise children near the pool, canals, stairs and any balcony. The home is not childproofed.
  • No commercial photoshoots or filming without approval.
Serious breaches (parties, damage, illegal activity, undeclared extra guests) can mean immediate eviction, loss of the security deposit, and a report to Airbnb.

On your doorstep

The pool & deck

The pool is for booked guests only. Rinse off sunscreen before a swim if you can, leave furniture where you found it, and never leave children unattended — not even “just for a minute.”

The waterways

Marina da Gama sits on canals linked to Zandvlei. Kayaks, SUPs and pedalos are how locals get around at sunset. If a craft is provided with the house, use it at your own risk, wear a life jacket, and stay off the main estuary mouth in wind or after rain.

Birds & otters

Herons, flamingos (seasonal), kingfishers, cormorants and the odd Cape clawless otter use the vlei. Please don’t feed wildlife, and keep noise down at dawn and dusk.

Courtyard & walks

Eat outside at the braai table. Park Island (no cars) and the Zandvlei paths are the easiest family walks. Muizenberg mountain trails start a short drive away.

Open Zandvlei site

Zandvlei Estuary Nature Reserve

Next door · free entry · bird hides, walks, kayaking with permit

A 300‑hectare wetland on your doorstep — part of False Bay Ecology Park. More than 150 bird species, rare fynbos and a working estuary that cleans water and buffers floods. Picnic, walk the Muizenberg circular paths, or watch flamingos when water levels allow.

Address: Park Island / Coniston area, Marina da Gama
Web: zandvleitrust.org.za · City reserve enquiries 021 444 1489

Open Muizenberg map

Muizenberg Beach & Surfers’ Corner

± 5–8 min drive · Blue Flag beach · beginner surf

False Bay’s friendliest beach: warmer water than the Atlantic side, gentle summer waves, and the famous rainbow Victorian changing huts. Surf schools at Surfers’ Corner hire boards and wetsuits. Pavilion pool, slides and cafés when you want a break from sand. Watch for bluebottles in season; lifeguards patrol in peak months.

Address: Beach Road, Muizenberg · Pavilion 021 788 1929

Open Kalk Bay guide

Kalk Bay village & harbour

± 10–15 min drive · seafood, seals, galleries

A working harbour where the boats still come in with fish and calamari. Watch Cape fur seals in the water, browse Main Road’s bookshops and ceramic studios, then eat with a mountain-and-bay view. The coastal Metrorail stop makes it an easy car-free outing if the trains are running.

Web: capetown.travel/kalk-bay

Open SANParks

Boulders Beach penguin colony

± 20–30 min drive · SANParks conservation fee

A sheltered granite cove in Simon’s Town with a wild colony of endangered African penguins. Stay on the boardwalks, do not touch or feed them (beaks are sharp), and swim only where signed. Combine with a stroll through Simon’s Town and a cone from a harbour café.

Address: Kleintuin Road, Simon’s Town · 021 786 2329
Web: sanparks.org

Open Cape Point

Cape Point & Cape of Good Hope

± 35–50 min drive · Table Mountain National Park

Rugged cliffs, fynbos, baboons, ostrich and the Flying Dutchman funicular up to the lighthouse. This is not where the oceans “meet” on a map, but the views feel like the end of Africa. Keep food locked away from baboons. Conservation fees apply; hours are shorter in winter.

Web: capepoint.co.za · Gate 021 780 9526

Open Kirstenbosch

Kirstenbosch National Botanical Garden

± 20–25 min drive · UNESCO Cape Floral Region

South Africa’s showcase garden on the eastern slopes of Table Mountain: proteas, cycads, the Boomslang canopy walkway, picnic lawns and summer sunset concerts. Spring wildflowers are the peak. Wheelchair-friendly routes and good on-site cafés.

Address: Rhodes Drive, Newlands · 021 799 8782
Web: sanbi.org/kirstenbosch

Open wine route

Constantia Wine Route

± 10–20 min drive · oldest wine region in South Africa

Cool-climate Sauvignon Blanc country in a green valley behind the mountain. Don’t miss Groot Constantia (museum + tastings), Klein Constantia (Vin de Constance) and a long lunch at Steenberg or Buitenverwachting. Book tastings on weekends. Nominate a driver.

Web: constantiawineroute.com
Groot Constantia 021 794 5128 · Klein Constantia 021 794 5188
